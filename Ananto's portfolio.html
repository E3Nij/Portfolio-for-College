The short version

Born in Chuadanga in 2005, raised across Kushtia, Dhaka and Beanibazar, Sylhet. Lost my father, a government agricultural officer, in 2017 when I was twelve, a stroke took him in six days. Everything after that was rebuilt from scratch: household finances, my father's pension paperwork, and eventually my own academic path, which went from top-ten student, to a collapse in grades, to a full year working ten-hour shifts as a computer operator, to finding my way back into a classroom with a completely new stream of study and a much clearer sense of why I was there. Along the way I kept building things anyway, a science project that survived six years of contests, a debate career, a national cadet corps rank, and a design and technology practice that started as a childhood obsession with computers.

Somewhere between homework and household bills, I turned into this guy 👇
